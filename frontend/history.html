Background History

SAMTECH SECURITY was founded by SAMWEL OCHIENG OKELLO with the vision of building a modern cybersecurity company capable of protecting individuals, businesses, and organizations from the rapidly growing digital threats affecting today's online world.

The company began as a small technology initiative focused on ethical hacking, website security, and cybersecurity awareness. Over time, SAMTECH SECURITY evolved into a broader cybersecurity and digital protection platform designed to provide enterprise-level security solutions for modern digital environments.
